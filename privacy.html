Privacy Policy

What we collect when you contact us, why, and how to ask us to change or delete it.

Last updated: October 24, 2026

Keys View Construction Group ("Keys View," "we," "us") operates thekeysview.com. This policy explains how we handle information you share with us through this website, by email or by phone.

Information we collect

Information you give us. When you fill out the estimate request form, we collect your name, phone number, email address, property address, the type of project, your timeline, how you prefer to be contacted, and anything you write in the message box. If you email or call us, we receive whatever you choose to share, such as violation notices, permit numbers or photos.

Information collected automatically. This website does not use cookies, analytics or advertising trackers. Like any website, the companies that host it and deliver its fonts (see below) may log basic technical details such as your IP address and browser type to keep their services running and secure.

How we use it

  • To respond to your request and answer your questions.
  • To schedule a walkthrough and prepare a scope, estimate and timeline.
  • To carry out and manage the work if you hire us, including permits, inspections and closeout.
  • To keep business records and meet legal obligations.

If you give us your phone number, we may call or text you about your request or project. We do not send marketing texts, and you can ask us to stop texting at any time.

How we share it

We do not sell your personal information. We share it only with the service providers that help us run this website and our business, and only as needed:

  • Web3Forms delivers estimate request form submissions to our email.
  • Google Workspace provides our business email.
  • GitHub Pages hosts this website.
  • Google Fonts provides the typefaces on this website.

When needed for your project, we may share relevant details with permitting offices, inspectors, code enforcement, or subcontractors working on your job. We may also disclose information if required by law or to protect our rights.

How long we keep it

We keep inquiry information for as long as needed to respond and follow up. If you become a client, we keep project records for as long as required for warranty, tax, licensing and legal purposes.

Your choices

You can ask us to see, correct or delete the personal information we hold about you by emailing KPowell@TheKeysView.com. We will respond within a reasonable time. We may keep records we are legally required to retain.

Do Not Track. Because this website does not track visitors across sites, it does not respond differently to browser "Do Not Track" signals.

Security

This website uses an encrypted (HTTPS) connection, and we limit access to your information to the people who need it to serve you. No method of sending or storing information online is completely secure, so please do not send sensitive financial information through the form.

Children

This website is intended for adults. We do not knowingly collect information from children under 13.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. The "Last updated" date at the top shows when it last changed.
